Challenges & Industry Motivation

The Problem

Organizations often manage IT assets and employee support requests through emails, spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

01

Poor Ticket Visibility

Support issues remain untracked across disparate email chains with no unified progress tracking.

02

Delayed Issue Resolution

Lack of escalation protocols and priority metrics leads to bottlenecks and service delays.

03

Difficulty Tracking IT Assets

Physical hardware locations, serial numbers, and assigned employees are fragmented or lost.

04

Inefficient Technician Assignment

Tickets are manually distributed without balancing workload or technician specializations.

05

Missing Maintenance & Warranties

Unrecorded warranty periods cause missed vendor claims and unexpected hardware repair expenses.

06

Lack of Centralized Reporting

Absence of relational audit logs prevents analysis of recurring faults and operational metrics.

“A centralized database-driven system is required to connect IT support operations with asset management.”
Project Goals & Capabilities

What Are We Building?

Click any goal card to reveal technical details

A reliable relational platform designed to unify daily service desk workflows with physical hardware lifecycle management.

Goal 01

Centralize IT Support Ticket Management

Unified repository for raising, categorizing, and prioritizing enterprise user issues.

Eliminates fragmented communication by logging requests into structured tables with automated sequencing and priority triage.
Goal 02

Maintain Records of Organizational Assets

Catalog physical devices, hardware specifications, asset tags, and department ownership.

Establishes single-source custody for equipment, tying unique asset tags and serials directly to departmental staff.
Goal 03

Manage Incidents & Service Requests

Differentiate critical unplanned system outages from standard employee hardware/access requests.

Separates urgent failure escalations from routine provisioning requests to enforce precise SLA resolution paths.
Goal 04

Assign Tickets to Appropriate Technicians

Structured delegation ensuring accountability and skill-matched technical troubleshooting.

Enforces assignment accountability via technician foreign keys, preventing neglected tickets and balancing queue loads.
Goal 05

Track Status & Resolution History

Capture immutable chronological status audit trails from intake to resolution closure.

Maintains full state audit logs in `status_histories`, recording timestamps and user actions from intake to resolution.
Goal 06

Monitor Warranties & Maintenance

Track active vendor warranty validity periods, repair descriptions, and servicing expenditures.

Prevents lapses in manufacturer coverage and maintains complete expense tracking across physical repairs.
Goal 07

Generate Meaningful Reports and Insights

Execute multi-table relational aggregations to evaluate mean time to resolution (MTTR), technician workload, and high-frequency asset hardware failures.

Unlocks automated SQL aggregations for MTTR benchmarks, recurring equipment defects, and department support bottlenecks.
System Capabilities & Specifications

Functional Requirements

Core functional specifications governing ticketing workflows, hardware asset tracking, and relational data integrity.

FR-01

Ticket Lifecycle Management

Create, categorize, and prioritize issues with automated classification into Incident (outage) vs Service Request (provisioning).

FR-02

Technician Assignment Engine

Assign pending tickets to specialized support staff with foreign key validation and automatic status transition to In Progress.

FR-03

Status Lifecycle & Audit Logging

Track immutable chronological transitions (Open → In Progress → Pending → Resolved → Closed) in status_histories.

FR-04

Resolution Verification

Record resolution descriptions, timestamps, and technician sign-offs while enforcing database constraints against duplicate resolutions.

FR-05

Hardware Asset Inventory

Catalog enterprise devices with unique asset tags, serial numbers, categories, departmental locations, and designated user ownership.

FR-06

Maintenance & Expense Records

Log servicing dates, repair actions, vendor contracts, and expenditure costs directly associated with physical equipment.

FR-07

Relational Reporting & Analytics

Execute multi-table relational aggregations to evaluate mean time to resolution (MTTR), technician queue metrics, high-failure hardware components, and upcoming warranty expiries.
