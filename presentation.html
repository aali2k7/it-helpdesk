Challenges & Industry Motivation

The Problem

Organizations often manage IT assets and employee support requests through emails, spreadsheets and disconnected systems.

01

Poor Ticket Visibility

Support issues remain untracked across disparate email chains with no unified progress tracking.

02

Delayed Issue Resolution

Lack of escalation protocols and priority metrics leads to bottlenecks and service delays.

03

Difficulty Tracking IT Assets

Physical hardware locations, serial numbers, and assigned employees are fragmented or lost.

04

Inefficient Technician Assignment

Tickets are manually distributed without balancing workload or technician specializations.

05

Missing Maintenance & Warranties

Unrecorded warranty periods cause missed vendor claims and unexpected hardware repair expenses.

06

Lack of Centralized Reporting

Absence of relational audit logs prevents analysis of recurring faults and operational metrics.

“A centralized database-driven system is required to connect IT support operations with asset management.”
Project Goals & Capabilities

What Are We Building?

A reliable relational platform designed to unify daily service desk workflows with physical hardware lifecycle management.

Goal 01

Centralize IT Support Ticket Management

Unified repository for raising, categorizing, and prioritizing enterprise user issues.

Goal 02

Maintain Records of Organizational Assets

Catalog physical devices, hardware specifications, asset tags, and department ownership.

Goal 03

Manage Incidents & Service Requests

Differentiate critical unplanned system outages from standard employee hardware/access requests.

Goal 04

Assign Tickets to Appropriate Technicians

Structured delegation ensuring accountability and skill-matched technical troubleshooting.

Goal 05

Track Status & Resolution History

Capture immutable chronological status audit trails from intake to resolution closure.

Goal 06

Monitor Warranties & Maintenance

Track active vendor warranty validity periods, repair descriptions, and servicing expenditures.

Goal 07

Generate Meaningful Reports and Insights

Execute multi-table relational aggregations to evaluate mean time to resolution (MTTR), technician workload, and high-frequency asset hardware failures.
